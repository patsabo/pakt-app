Community rules

Friends on Pakt see each other's names and the goals they share. Keep it kind.

Last updated 11 October 2026.

Not allowed

  • Harassment, bullying, threats or intimidation of anyone.
  • Hate against people for who they are: ethnicity, nationality, religion, disability, sex, gender identity or sexual orientation.
  • Sexual content, and anything sexual involving someone under 18.
  • Goals that encourage self-harm, dangerous dieting or violence, or that are about controlling another person.
  • Spam, scams, advertising and links in names.
  • Pretending to be someone else.

What you can do

Open a friend from the town hall to remove them, block them, or report them. A block ends the friendship, and neither of you can add the other until you lift it. A report goes to the person who runs Pakt and is looked at within a day; nobody is told who reported them.

What happens

A goal that breaks these rules is hidden from friends. An account that breaks them is closed. Threats to someone's safety may be reported to the police.

Contact

Write to patrick.alfei.sabo@gmail.com. If you or someone else is in danger, call your local emergency number.
