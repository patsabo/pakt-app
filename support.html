Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Write to patrick.alfei.sabo@gmail.com. You'll get an answer within a few days.

Questions

Do I need an account?

No. Pakt opens without one, and your drafts stay on the phone. When you sign your first contract, Pakt asks you to sign in with Apple, so your city is kept safe and follows you to a new phone.

How do I get my data out?

Open the town hall (the settings), then Privacy and consent, and choose Export your data as JSON. You get every contract and its history as a JSON file.

How do I delete my account?

Open the town hall, then Account, and choose Delete account. Your account, every contract and your city are deleted at once, on the phone and on the server. If you have the yearly plan, cancel it in the App Store first: Apple bills it, and deleting your account doesn't cancel it.

Can I change a contract after signing?

For 24 hours after signing you can withdraw it, and it disappears without a trace. After that, the outcome, the deadline and the why are locked, but your plan, milestones and habits stay yours to change. Once per contract, you can amend the deadline or the outcome, and the contract keeps a note of it.

What does the coach do with my text?

Only when you ask, the coach sends the draft you're writing to Anthropic, the company whose Claude model reads it, and shows you its suggestions. It isn't used to train their models. You can turn the coach off in the town hall.

How do I manage or cancel the yearly plan?

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Pakt. In the app, the town hall's Plan section has a link there too, and Restore purchases if you bought the plan on another phone.

Something looks wrong in my city

Write to us with a screenshot and what you expected to see. It helps a lot.
